Virtual HR — Guidelines & Policy Support

Problem

Employees often struggle to find specific answers within lengthy company policy documents, leading to wasted time and potential compliance issues.

Solution

An AI-based chatbot that uses Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) to intelligently search through uploaded company documents and provide accurate, context-aware answers to employee questions.

Technology
Python RAG AI APIs Document Processing Chatbot
Features
  • Upload and process company policy documents
  • Natural language question answering
  • Context-aware responses using RAG
  • Source document references in answers
  • AI API integration for intelligent responses
Goal

To help employees quickly access accurate information from company policies and guidelines, improving efficiency and compliance.
